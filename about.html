menu_book The Raniganj Genesis

Two street-food purists, one scalding hot plancha, and zero compromises.

We started Burger University with a blunt realization: Raniganj deserved real smash burgers with paper-thin, crispy lacy edges, authentic steaming mountain broths, and fried chicken that holds its violent crunch down to the absolute last bite.

Fast-casual chains had made food clinical, predictable, and frozen. We chose the opposite route: fresh artisanal brioche baked daily, beef and chicken coarsely ground within hours of service, fiery home-brewed chili dabs, and hand-crimped Darjeeling-style momos.

No campus degrees needed—just a masterclass in blistering heat, seasoned fat, and raw street soul.

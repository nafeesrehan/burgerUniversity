Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our sourcing, delivery boundaries, and bespoke event packages.

moped

Do you deliver across Raniganj?

Yes, absolutely! We run direct priority delivery for nearby neighborhoods via our in-house delivery fleet. You can also place instant orders through Swiggy and Zomato within an 8km service radius of NSB Road.

check_circle Direct + Swiggy / Zomato
celebration

Do you cater private events & birthdays?

Yes! We build custom sliders, smash burger buffets, and live live-griddle food truck pop-ups for birthdays, office milestones, and late-night celebrations. Reach out via the form for custom menus.

check_circle Live Grill & Buffet Setups
restaurant_menu

Are vegetarian and halal options available?

Yes, 100%. We maintain strictly segregated cooking griddles and fryer oil for all vegetarian patties (including Paneer Crispers & Truffle Potato). All poultry and meats are 100% halal-certified.
